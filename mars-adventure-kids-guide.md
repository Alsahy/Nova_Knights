# 🚀 Mars Base Adventure: What Could Happen?
### A guide for young astronauts

Hello, Commander! 👋

You are in charge of a small base on **Mars** with **4 astronauts**.
Every day, something new can happen. **You choose what to do.**
But be careful: **every choice costs something**, just like for real astronauts.

This guide tells you about your crew, your base, the missions you can play, and all the surprises that **might** happen.

---

## 🔴 Welcome to Mars!

- 🌅 A day on Mars is called a **sol**. It lasts 24 hours and 39 minutes, a little longer than a day on Earth.
- 🥶 Mars is **very cold**. At night it can drop to −89 °C, colder than any freezer!
- 💨 The air is thin and is mostly **carbon dioxide**. You can't breathe it, so you need a spacesuit outside.
- 🪶 Gravity is weaker. If you weigh 30 kg on Earth, you would weigh only about 11 kg on Mars!
- 🌪️ Mars has **dust storms**, sometimes big enough to cover the whole planet.
- 📡 A message to Earth takes **3 to 22 minutes** to arrive. You can't have a live phone call!

---

## 🎲 Every adventure is different!

Every day on Mars starts with a **surprise**. You have **10 seconds** to pick what to do. Too slow? Your crew picks for you, and not in a good way!

- 🎲 There are **14 surprises**, shuffled like a deck of cards. Play again and you get a new story! 🎉
- ☀️ Some surprises come from **the real Sun**! The game replays real solar storms from May 2024, using NASA's space-weather data.
- 🔗 Some surprises only happen after something else. A flash on the Sun can bring a radiation storm!
- 🛠️ After the surprise, give each astronaut **one job**. Then night comes and the next day begins.

## 🧑‍🚀 Meet your crew

| Card | Job | Super power |
|---|---|---|
| 🧑‍✈️ **Manager** | The leader | Can **peek** at tomorrow's surprise and **cheer up** a tired teammate |
| 🔧 **Engineer** | The fixer | Fixes machines **faster** and builds shields |
| 🩺 **Medical** | The doctor | Heals astronauts and can **see exactly** how full the radiation badges are |
| 🔬 **Researcher** | The scientist | Gets **double science stars** ⭐ on lab and rock-study jobs, and is great with plants |

🌍 **True story:** NASA's practice Mars crew, who lived for 378 days in a pretend Mars house on Earth, had these same 4 jobs!

### Your crew shares 5 bars

Your 4 astronauts are a team, so they **share** one set of bars. When one astronaut works hard, the whole crew's energy goes down!

| Bar | What it means | Keep it… |
|---|---|---|
| 🍽️ **Tummy** | Are they fed? | FULL |
| 💧 **Water** | Are they drinking enough? | FULL |
| 🫁 **Air** | Do they have air? (This matters most outside, in a spacesuit.) | FULL |
| ⚡ **Energy** | How much can they work? | FULL |
| ☢️ **Radiation badge** | How many space rays have hit your crew? | **LOW!** It never goes down |

- If a bar (except the badge) goes **empty**, one astronaut has to rest in **Sick Bay** for 2 sols.
- The **radiation badge** goes 🟢 **green** → 🟡 **yellow** → 🔴 **red** as it fills up.
- If the **radiation badge** gets full, the whole crew must **stay inside** for the rest of the mission.

## 🏠 Your Mars base

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🫁 **Life Support** | Makes **air** (from water!), cleans the air, and recycles water |
| 🛡️ **Shield** | Walls of **Mars soil** and **water bags** that block space rays |
| ⚡ **Power** | **Solar panels**, a **battery**, and a small **reactor** |
| 🔬 **Lab** | Where you do science and earn **science stars ⭐** (your mission goal!) |

**Supplies:** 🥫 food · 💧 water · 🫧 oxygen tanks · 🔩 spare parts · 🩹 medical kits

## 📏 The 5 golden rules

1. 💰 **Nothing is free.** Every choice costs energy, power, water, time… something!
2. ⏳ **Some things happen later.** A choice today can surprise you tomorrow.
3. 🎲 **Some things are a "maybe".** Risky choices might work… or not.
4. 💚 **Nobody gets hurt for real.** If things go badly, Mission Control brings everyone home safely.
5. 🧠 **Every mistake teaches you something.** At the end you'll see what happened and what real NASA teams do.

### ☀️ Surprises from the Sun

**🌟 Flash on the Sun!**
The Sun makes a super-bright flash. Tiny, super-fast bits might follow.
**You can…** bring everyone inside · finish your outside job really fast · get the shelter ready
🌍 **True story:** Flares come in sizes A, B, C, M and X. Each one is 10 times stronger than the one before!

**☢️ Radiation Storm!**
Invisible bits from the Sun are here! They can fill up your radiation badges.
**You can…** everyone into the shelter (no work today) · build a wall of water bags · keep working (badges go up)
🌍 **True story:** In May 2024, the Curiosity rover on Mars measured its biggest radiation jump in 12 years. That's like getting 30 X-rays!

**🌪️ Storm Cloud Coming**
The Sun threw a giant cloud into space. It reaches Mars in a few sols!
**You can…** pile more soil on the roof · do extra science before it arrives · charge all the batteries
🌍 **True story:** NASA computers can predict when these clouds will reach Mars.

**💥 The Storm Cloud Hits**
The cloud is here! Radios crackle and the shields get tested.
**You can…** turn the radios off and wait · keep the radios on (the antenna might get knocked) · measure the storm for science
🌍 **True story:** In May 2024, many of these clouds hit Earth and made the biggest space storm in 20 years.

**😎 Quiet Sun Day**
The Sun is calm. A great day to go outside!
**You can…** go on a big science walk · fix things outside · give everyone a rest day
🌍 **True story:** Even on calm days, tiny space rays keep coming. On Mars you get about a third of what you'd get flying through space.

**📈 Rays Add Up** 🎓
Radiation badges fill up a little every sol. Who goes outside next?
**You can…** take turns going outside · send the expert again · add another layer of soil to the walls
🌍 **True story:** NASA has a lifetime limit for how much radiation an astronaut can get.

**🔴 Badge Turning Red**
The crew's radiation badge is almost full!
**You can…** do only inside jobs for a while · get a doctor's check-up · let them do one last important trip (risky!)
🌍 **True story:** Real astronauts wear radiation badges, just like in the game.

**🌍✅ Earth Is Safe, Mars Is Not** 🎓
Earth says "no problem"… but the Sun's cloud is heading for Mars!
**You can…** trust the Mars forecast and get ready · think "Earth is fine, so are we" · ask the satellites around Mars to measure it
🌍 **True story:** Sometimes NASA's model says a cloud will miss Earth but hit Mars. Earth and Mars are in different places!

**📨 Message from Space-Weather Forecasters** 🎓
Real forecasters sent a message about the Sun.
**You can…** follow their advice · keep your plan · ask Earth what it means (the answer comes tomorrow)
🌍 **True story:** NASA has a real team that watches the Sun for trips to the Moon and Mars.

**📟 Radiation Sensor Broken** 🎓
You can't see storms coming!
**You can…** fix the sensor · use Earth's warnings (they arrive late) · stay inside more just in case
🌍 **True story:** NASA's Orion spaceship has radiation sensors that tell astronauts when to hide.

### ⚡ Power surprises

**🌡️ Hot Machine!**
The battery box is getting too hot!
**You can…** leave it running (it might go POP and break!) · cool it with water · switch it off to cool down
🌍 **True story:** Spaceships use cooling pipes full of liquid to carry heat away.

**🟫 Dusty Solar Panels**
Dust covers the solar panels. Less power every sol!
**You can…** go out and brush them · wait for a lucky wind · tilt the panels from inside
🌍 **True story:** The Spirit rover's panels got so dusty that only a quarter of the sunlight got through.

**🌪️✨ Lucky Wind!**
A whirlwind blew the dust off your panels. Extra power!
**You can…** charge the batteries · power a big experiment · give the plants extra light
🌍 **True story:** In 2009, a Martian wind cleaned the Spirit rover's panels and gave it more power.

**🌫️ Dust Storm Coming**
Satellites see a dust storm. Soon the Sun will be hard to see.
**You can…** fill the batteries now · finish outside jobs first · keep the normal plan
🌍 **True story:** The Opportunity rover needed a fairly clear sky to recharge its batteries.

**🌑 Planet-Wide Dust Storm** 🎓
Dust covers ALL of Mars. Day looks like night!
**You can…** go into sleep mode · use the reactor only for air · keep one experiment running
🌍 **True story:** In 2018, a giant dust storm cut Opportunity's power by 97%. It never woke up again.

**📉 Power Fading Slowly** 🎓
Dust keeps piling up, and there's a little less power each sol.
**You can…** keep only your most important machine on · do a big cleaning walk with 2 astronauts · run things part-time
🌍 **True story:** Dust slowly covered the InSight lander's solar panels, and its mission ended in 2022.

**🥶 Freezing Night**
Tonight is super cold!
**You can…** turn the heaters up · put everyone in one warm room · wrap the pipes like a blanket
🌍 **True story:** A NASA lander measured −89 °C on a Mars night!

**🔌 Not Enough Power!**
You need more power than you have. Something must be unplugged!
**You can…** unplug the lab · unplug the plant lights · turn down the air machines (risky!)
🌍 **True story:** Mars gets less than half the sunlight Earth gets.

**✂️ Cut Cable**
A power cable got cut. Half your power is gone!
**You can…** fix it outside · use the spare cable · live with half power
🌍 **True story:** The biggest marsquake ever measured shook the ground for hours.

**⚛️ Pick Your Power Plant** 🎓
How will your base get its power?
**You can…** build a big solar farm · bring a bigger reactor · put the reactor close to the base (easy to fix, more radiation)
🌍 **True story:** NASA is designing a small reactor that could power a base for 10 years.

### 🫁 Air & water surprises

**😮‍💨 Stuffy Air**
The air feels stuffy and people get headaches. Too much breathed-out air (CO₂)!
**You can…** fix the air cleaner · use spare filters · have everyone rest so they breathe less
🌍 **True story:** Each astronaut breathes out about 1 kg of CO₂ every day!

**🔲⭕ Square Filter, Round Hole**
The spare filters are the wrong shape!
**You can…** build an adapter together · ask Mission Control for a plan (it arrives tomorrow) · use oxygen tanks
🌍 **True story:** On Apollo 13, astronauts built an adapter from bags, tape and a book cover, and it saved them!

**🫧 Oxygen Maker Stopped**
The machine that makes air from water stopped!
**You can…** repair it · light an oxygen candle (tiny chance of smoke!) · breathe from stored tanks
🌍 **True story:** Each person needs about 0.8 kg of oxygen every day.

**💨 Smoke!**
The smoke alarm is beeping! The oxygen candle is sparking.
**You can…** put on masks and use the extinguisher · close the door to that room
🌍 **True story:** In 1997, an oxygen candle caught fire on the Mir space station. The crew put it out and everyone was OK.

**🚰 Water Recycler Clogged**
The machine that cleans water is clogged!
**You can…** swap the filter · drink from the reserve · drink a little less today
🌍 **True story:** The space station turns sweat, breath and even pee back into clean drinking water!

**🔧 Water-Saving Gadget** 🎓
You can install a gadget that saves even more water.
**You can…** install it now · skip it and do science · install it slowly over 2 sols
🌍 **True story:** A new gadget helped the space station recycle 98% of its water.

**🎈 Tiny Leak**
Hissss! Air is slowly leaking out.
**You can…** find it and patch it · close that room · add air and wait
🌍 **True story:** Tiny space rocks can punch little holes in spaceships.

**❄️ Cooling Leak** 🎓
The cooling liquid is leaking. The base will slowly get too hot inside!
**You can…** turn off machines you don't need · fix it on a spacewalk · run hot and hope
🌍 **True story:** In 2022, a tiny hole let all the cooling liquid leak out of a Soyuz spaceship in about 18 hours.

**⚖️ Air or Water?**
You make air by splitting water, but water is running low!
**You can…** keep making air · use oxygen tanks instead · go dig for ice
🌍 **True story:** Space stations really do make their oxygen by splitting water.

**🧊 Ice Found!**
There's ice under the dust! It can become water, and even air.
**You can…** dig it up and melt it · study it for science · save it for later
🌍 **True story:** In 2008, NASA's Phoenix lander found ice on Mars!

**🔴🫧 Make Air from Mars Air**
Mars air is mostly CO₂. A machine can pull oxygen out of it!
**You can…** run the oxygen machine · save the power for the lab · test it for science
🌍 **True story:** The MOXIE machine on Mars made oxygen. In total, about what a small dog breathes in 10 hours!

### 🌱 Food surprises

**🌱 What Should We Plant?**
Pick seeds for your greenhouse!
**You can…** plant lettuce (fast, small) · plant chile peppers (slow, big) · plant radishes (in between)
🌍 **True story:** Astronauts grew lettuce in space, from seed to salad in 33 days!

**🐝 Flowers Need Help** 🎓
There are no bees on Mars! Pepper flowers need help to make peppers.
**You can…** help them by hand · turn the fans up · skip it
🌍 **True story:** Astronauts helped space peppers by hand and with fans.

**🥫 Old Food Losing Vitamins** 🎓
Packed food slowly loses its vitamins. The crew feels tired.
**You can…** eat fresh greens · take the doctor's vitamins · ignore it
🌍 **True story:** After about a year, packed space food has lost most of its vitamin C.

**📦 Pack the Cargo**
Before you go, pick ONE extra crate!
**You can…** take a food crate · take a spare-parts crate · take a science-kit crate
🌍 **True story:** Rockets can only leave for Mars about every 26 months, so you live with what you bring!

**🌮 Taco Night!**
The peppers are ripe!
**You can…** throw a taco party · save them for later · send some to scientists
🌍 **True story:** In 2021, astronauts made tacos with peppers they grew in space!

**🍽️ Food Running Low**
Only a few days of food left!
**You can…** eat smaller meals · make the greenhouse grow faster · open the emergency food box
🌍 **True story:** An astronaut needs about 3,000 calories a day.

**🔴🌱 Plants in Mars Soil?** 🎓
There's free soil outside, but is it safe?
**You can…** test it first · keep using plant pillows · plant in it right away (risky!)
🌍 **True story:** Mars soil has a salt called perchlorate that's bad for people.

### 🧑‍🚀 Crew surprises

**🏋️ Exercise or Experiment?**
Muscles and bones get weaker in low gravity.
**You can…** have everyone exercise · skip it for science · let the doctor plan short workouts
🌍 **True story:** On the space station, astronauts exercise about 2 hours every day to keep their bones strong.

**😴 Can't Sleep**
An astronaut can't fall asleep.
**You can…** let them sleep in · try the doctor's sleep routine · make them work anyway
🌍 **True story:** Astronauts are given 8.5 hours to sleep, but usually sleep only about 6.

**🏠 Missing Home**
An astronaut misses their family.
**You can…** send a video home · have a crew game night · keep busy with work
🌍 **True story:** NASA's practice Mars crew had to wait up to 22 minutes for their messages to reach Earth.

**🤧 Dust Sneezes**
Dust came inside on the spacesuits. Achoo!
**You can…** clean the suits in the airlock · run the air filters extra · ignore it
🌍 **True story:** On the Moon, astronaut Harrison Schmitt got "Moon hay fever" from dust!

**🪖 Water in the Helmet!**
Water is floating inside an astronaut's helmet during a spacewalk!
**You can…** come back inside right now · have a buddy walk them back · finish the job first (a bad idea!)
🌍 **True story:** In 2013, this really happened to astronaut Luca Parmitano. NASA brought him back inside safely.

**🦶 Slip on a Rock**
An astronaut slipped outside and hurt their ankle.
**You can…** let the doctor treat it · rest for 2 sols · tape it and keep going (risky!)
🌍 **True story:** Mars gravity is only about a third of Earth's, so walking feels very different.

**🥱 Too Tired to Work**
The crew is almost out of energy!
**You can…** give them a day off · have others do the job · give them an energy snack
🌍 **True story:** Tired astronauts find their work harder, just like tired kids!

**👓 Blurry Vision** 🎓
An astronaut's eyes are a bit blurry.
**You can…** do an eye check · give them jobs without screens · ignore it
🌍 **True story:** In low gravity, body fluids float up toward the head, and that can change astronauts' eyes.

Cameras see an amazing rock… but it's far away!

**🌋 Marsquake!**

## 💡 Tips from Mission Control

- 🔋 **Save power on good days.** Bad days always come.
- 🛡️ **Build shields early.** Some Sun storms give you no warning at all!
- 🔄 **Take turns going outside**, so nobody's radiation badge fills up too fast.
- 🔩 **Don't use up all your spare parts.** You'll need them later.
- 😴 **Rest is not lazy.** A tired crew makes mistakes.
- 🧠 **If something goes wrong, read the "What happened" screen.** That's where you learn the most!
